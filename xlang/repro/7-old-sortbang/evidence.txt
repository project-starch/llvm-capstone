=== why both tools are blind ===
The pre-fix guard is `if (RARRAY_PTR(ary) != p) raise "array modified during
sort"`. ASan and valgrind both replace realloc with an always-moving
implementation, so the pointer changes, the guard fires, and the defect never
occurs. The system allocator satisfies the 100 -> 2 element shrink IN PLACE:
the pointer is unchanged, the guard passes, and heap sort keeps indexing a
buffer that is now far smaller.

The sanitizer does not fail to observe the defect. It PREVENTS it, by changing
the allocator behaviour the defect depends on. See target.md.
