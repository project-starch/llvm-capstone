## readings (not predictions; written after the boot)
- 4 of the 5 OFF cells come from the vm step. It exits 139 with cause 15 at its arena 0x3fb8500000 + 0x2000
  (p[8192]), and the module's own S-24 bound line ("resolved 65 times with no change") is in the step text.
  CORRECTED (Addendum 17): the attribution to S-24 (ISSUES.md) is by shape and bound line only. The bound line reads
  `pte rc=-38 ... dirty=-1` because resolve_pte was off, so S-24's defining value (a PTE with V R W A set and D = 0)
  was not read. Boot b4-t1c (resolve_dirty=1 resolve_pte=1) settles it. The twin cannot show S-24. Its fix
  (resolve_dirty) defaults off, and the kit loaded the module with no parameters.
  The extra fault shifts fault_sites by one (nested_denial_site, vm_denial_sites);
  the missing vm stats line leaves 10 CAPSTONE_VM_STATS lines where 11 are expected (linux_pages_released,
  linux_demand_faults). The pre-registration's error: a twin reference for a step with a known silicon-only issue.
- spin_entered: OPEN. run.sh kills the spin child after a QEMU-sized 'sleep 5', and /tmp/spin.out is empty. Candidate:
  launches on this board take 3.7-8.4 s (launch_ns), against 8-22 ms on the twin. Against it: vp2 boots b4c and b5
  read spin_entered true with the same sleep. Settled by a rerun with a longer wait.
