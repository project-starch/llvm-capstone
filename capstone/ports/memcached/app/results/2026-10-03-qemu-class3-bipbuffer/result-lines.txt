# Identical cursor AND identical bounds: both records are sub-allocations inside ONE malloc
# (bipbuf_new = malloc(sizeof(bipbuf_t) + size), flexible data[] at +0x20 after 16-byte alignment,
# so len-from-cursor is the whole 4096-byte data area). Per-object bounds on the OUTER allocation
# cannot separate the records -- which is what "nested allocator" means here. BUT the blindness is
# TEMPORAL, not spatial: both records occupy the SAME bytes, so even per-record bounds could not
# separate them. Nothing fires because no revocation event ever occurs.
