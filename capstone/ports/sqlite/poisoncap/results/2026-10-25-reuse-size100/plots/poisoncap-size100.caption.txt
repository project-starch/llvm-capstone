Address reuse: cumulative percentage of successful memsys5 allocations that return a previously freed start address within the indicated reuse distance. Reuse distance: allocations since the preceding free of that address, the reuse included (immediate reuse: 1); power-of-two bins, plotted at their inclusive upper edges. First-time allocations stay in the denominator.
