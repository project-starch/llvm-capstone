## Step 1b: the format in the core (design next)

slot 31} (55 -> 63 bits at about 70 sites), R-60's window limit and the genesis range under 2^38, and the decode shim
(every decoded capability NONLIN RWX). Site census and pre-registration to follow, on bitstream 1 + R-75 option (d).
