The images, the emulator records and the bitstream-independent predictions are the compiler lane's
pre-registration: `lane/compiler-p1-prereg` at `10c0e4fe`,
`docs/plans/2026-09-29-p1-cell6-board-prereg.md`, section "THE -O2 PAIR". This file adds only what
the board decides: platform, invocation, the order of the boots, and the readings specific to silicon.

## Platform

`caplifive_r42_6cbdaeeb4.bit` is resident, the platform of record by the lead's R-42 decision.

The R-43+R-45 build (capstone-ariane `0f5185a6d`) failed the synthesis row of its own acceptance:
- a different routed TIMING-23 arc;
- 52 false-path exceptions against 12;
- WNS −24.495 against −10.615.

The RTL lane withdrew it as a flash candidate, so P1 runs on R-42 rather than waiting for a redesign.

## Readings predicted on silicon

1. `RESULT k800 retval=4` from the relinked control at both ends of both boots. A failure makes that boot VOID, and it is the relinked control's fault, not the silicon's.
2. Cell 5:
   - `Verification Hash: 112006 38bb59fd`, HEAP 2097152;
   - Successful lookasides 25,010, Lookaside Slots Used 1;
   - no `sublet:` line;
   - no cause-25.
3. Native -O2: `112006 38bb59fd` and Successful lookasides 25,122, as in sw80a.
4. Cell 6, if it completes:
   - `112006 38bb59fd`, HEAP 1344064;
   - `sublet: split=5568 mrev=37970 delin=32569 revoke=37970 init=5401`, the emulator's `--stats` record;
   - Successful lookasides 25,010 and non-zero, which is the lead's 2026-09-25 condition read on silicon;
   - `SQ: released pool rc=1`;
   - probe `ALEN:00200000`, `RR/done`.
5. **Cell 6 may instead trap cause 25, and that would be R-43, not a P1 failure.** R-42 carries R-35's deny-on-miss, and R-43 false-denies a live capability once more than ~256 revocation ids are live. The R1 harness trapped 25 on R-42 in boots r42b3/r42b4. Cell 6's peak count of live Sublet ids is not known. A cause-25 is reported with mepc−DBAS and tval, and recorded as R-43 manifesting in the P1 workload. P1 ⑥ then waits for the R-43 redesign. Cell 5 performs no Sublet revocations and is not exposed.
6. Cycles are reported, not predicted. The band is CPI 3.2..4.9 against the emulator count, and the sw80a CPIs are the comparison: ⑤ -O2 3.527, native 3.56.

## What these boots do not establish

- One run per cell. This is not the five-run P1 timing protocol (`METHODS.md`).
- Cell 6 completing says nothing about R-43 in general, only that this workload stayed under the live-id threshold.
