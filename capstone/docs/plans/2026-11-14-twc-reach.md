*RTL lane, on the runtime lane's request after the per-instruction cost table: the cost of a miss on a page that
holds capabilities is the tag path, not the opcode. Prior art: `2026-11-13-tag-write-list.md` (this file's base
design: page bitmap, TWC, pending tag-write list) and spec-silicon `2026-11-12-tag-page-bitmap.md` (v2).*

## Why (the cost table, sim, fdk and the batch)

Extra cycles per op against the plain twin in the same page state: ldc +0.99 on a hit, +0.5 on a miss; stc +2.92
(fdk) / +1.69 (batch) on a hit; movc +1.61. The large term is per PAGE: on a page that has held a capability a load
miss costs +16.0 and a store miss +44.4 sim cycles whatever the opcode (cost-tab.S, capstone-ariane
perf/cost-tab-fdk / perf/cost-tab-batch). The TWC covered 32 KB of data, the same reach as the L1D; a tag write
invalidated its entry; and with a write-through L1D every store that drains on a marked page writes a tag byte unless
the TWC holds it with the same value. Sim cycles here come from `axi2mem`, which serves one AXI transaction at a time;
what transfers to silicon is the COUNT of tag reads and writes, hence the events.

## Audit (claim-auditor, on 54bdfd53c, by reading)

- REFUTED, fixed in bbfc6abc1: an install to the same index in the cycle before a store's merge latch leaves the
  registered lookup showing the store's word while the entry took another's; the merge then wrote one word's bytes
  under the other's atag and kept it valid. The merge now writes the atag too.
- REFUTED, NOT a lever in this bitstream: removing the TWC clear on a page fill. Tag words are not page-aligned
  (`CAP_TAG_MEM_BASE = 0xBC2D2D2D`), so a page's first and last tag words are shared with its neighbours and can be
  cached with the page's leftover bytes. Safe narrowing for later: invalidate only those two indices.
- SUPPORTED: config B is the base's behaviour apart from the events; no forward reference, no second driver, no loop.
- No page goes from marked to unmarked except at reset, which also resets the TWC.
- Found on the way, filed separately: ISSUES R-73 (the debug module's system-bus master writes DRAM around the adapter).

## Silicon (runtime lane)

mcf and gcc first, events 24-28 and the L1D-miss event read on both the batch and F. The unknown that decides
everything is mcf's ratio of tag reads to L1D misses on silicon. The reading under the current best hypothesis (the
TWC's 32 KB reach is the binding limit): on the batch, ev24 / (ev24 + ev25) near 1 on mcf; on F, a large fall in ev24
and ev26 at equal L1D misses. If ev24 is already small on the batch, the TWC was not the cost and F's cycles will not
move; the events then point at what was.
