## 3. The bounds encoder and decoder

- The resident encoder has no cursorless scheme (4a4261bb4): every capability rounds OUTWARD; R-11's truncation
  cannot occur. The resident DECODER (d34c26002 on the Sublet tree) has no cursorless branch either; the FP respin's
  decoder still has one (fp-fixes keeps `bounds.cursorless`, 5 references), so a software-compressed cursorless
  word decodes on the fallback bitstream and is NOT_CAP-shaped on the resident one. FINDING (compatibility): any
  software compressor (the runtime's compress_cap, the physical QEMU) must emit the 27-bit full word for the
  resident bitstream; check-repr's `--format full --renorm` is the model and the board lane's defaults carry it.
- R-54's renormalisation is correct on every representable region (bounds-model.py: 400,000 regions x 3 cursors
  cover; 8,259,510 regions equal between the exact and the residue form; the helper lane's 2,001,820-case
  measurement against QEMU's exact reference 0 mismatches). A region whose rounded top is 2^64 is unrepresentable
  under either form (half the span on the sum form, an empty capability on the residue form). Unreachable from
  genesis: CAPENTER's capabilities end at 2^40 at most (capstone_flu_unit.anvil MEM_END 64'h10000000000,
  INIT_DATA_END 64'h82000000), CDERIVE rounds outward by at most one granule, and the only instructions that can
  place a top there are the debug mints of 6.1 F-A. The model asserts both facts; the control fires.
- The encoder refuses nothing: CDERIVE, SHRINK, TIGHTEN and SPLIT round outward with no representability check
  (Q-15; QEMU's compact profile refuses with 29). Consequences carried by software: R-55 (a SPLIT's lower piece takes
  the rounded base as cursor; fixed in the monitor by explicit cursors), the carve overlap (a granule on each side
  of an unaligned carve point; the carve policy, OVERLAP 0 under check-repr, is the next monitor change), and the
  heap's over-coverage (cderive_child models it; every object below 4 KiB at 16-byte alignment is exact).
  FINDING (design decision, standing): whether the RTL should ever refuse an unrepresentable child (QEMU's 29) is a
  divergence the registry records as Q-15; the software side has chosen to live with rounding. The audit does not
  reopen it; it notes that `exceeds_parent` in cderive_child is the only check that a child does not leave its
  parent, and it is a model, not hardware.

## 6. The Sublet units, the registry, and the coverage inventory (the three delegated strands)

### 6.1 The Sublet units (rtl-oracle, read-only, files at d93a8d66b; file:line as reported, the two defects re-read here)

Confirmed by reading, no action needed:
- **CPMP invalidation covers CREVOKE.** `revnode_invalidation_valid_o = mem_rev_wr_req_valid && !node_wr_req[31]`
  (ex_stage.sv:1424-1425) fires on any rev-node write that clears `valid`; REVOKE's walk, DROP and CREVOKE's kill
  (the direct child and every swept descendant) all go through the same write path, and pmp_data_if.sv:94-107 clears
  the entry on the index. A CPMP entry installed from a derived child dies with the child.
- **CREVOKE ends grandchildren.** kill_req latches the child's depth as the bound and walks from the child's `next`
  (capstone_rev_node.anvil:368-414, the walk is REVOKE_NODE in kill mode); CDERIVE inserts a child right after its
  parent at depth + 1, so a parent's descendants are one contiguous deeper run. The parent's liveness is checked by
  the dyn unit's generation-checked query before kill_req, and issue_read_operands.sv:1558-1564 serialises every
  tree-mutating op behind the previous commit, which is what makes the index-only parent compare safe. If that
  serialisation were ever relaxed, a reused parent index could match by accident (marked by the oracle; stands).
- **Node death ends every capability on the node** (the NONLIN split's two halves; the query reads `(valid,
  generation)` only, rev_node.anvil:207-216), and **depth is never read by a validity check**, so MREV's deepening
  (even a double deepening after a flush-and-replay between its write pass and mrev_req, depth + 2 instead of + 1)
  cannot affect a younger load, a CPMP entry or a later walk's bound test (`depth <= bound`).
- **List order under the reachable sequence** SPLIT(N) -> S; MREV(N) -> H1; MREV(N) -> H2; DELIN(N); CDERIVE(N) -> C
  is `[S(d), H1(d), H2(d+1), N(d+2), C(d+3)]`: REVOKE(H2) ends N and C and never reaches S; REVOKE(H1) also sweeps the
  never-revoked H2, by design. (The literal "LINEAR split sibling and CDERIVE child on one node" cannot be built
  without a DELIN between, which is why the earlier "unreachable" claim was half right and R-21 made it wrong.)
- **R-45's list** carries CREVOKE at both commit_stage.sv sites (716-720, 792-793); CDERIVE, DELIN, SPLIT and MREV
  never clear a live node's `valid`, so they need no flush.
- **UNOPTFLAT 40** by name identical to 0568f93a9 at every Sublet step (the gate's REF unchanged, the by-name diff
  recorded in the step messages); none of the 40 names is in the rev-node or dyn unit.

Findings:
- **F-A (hazard, severity depends on the threat model; mechanism confirmed, decoder.sv:1125-1139 and
  capstone_flu_unit.anvil:400-434).** The five "Capstone Debug Instructions" (CAPCREATE, CAPTYPE, CAPNODE, CAPPERM,
  CAPBOUND) check nothing and are refused only inside a SUPERVISED domain (`sup_active_i && !debug_mode_i`). Any
  code outside supervision, at any privilege, can copy a live node id onto a fresh register (CAPNODE) and give it
  arbitrary bounds, permissions and type: a capability over everything, tied to a lifetime it did not earn. The
  academic spec does not define these instructions; the RTL comment calls them debug, the test headers "test
  instructions", and production software mints only through INIT / CDERIVE / MREV. On this project the domains
  that matter run supervised (the process ABI, B0), so the boundary that is defended holds; a plain-CALL domain is
  not defended against this. Consumers enumerated before recommending (grep of the five mnemonics): the runtime, the compiler
  (`llvm/lib/Target/RISCV`), the kernel module and the Sublet library have none; the monitor's two hits are comments;
  the bare arms, the s1gap/sublet images and the static-audit controls mint in M-mode; no S- or U-mode user was
  found. Recommendation: gate the group on M-mode (`priv_lvl_i == PRIV_LVL_M || debug_mode_i`) in the decoder, a
  one-condition change that no enumerated consumer would notice; directed
  test: a CAPCREATE in S-mode must raise 2, with the same instruction in M-mode as the control. Not an action of
  this audit; a decision for the lead.
- **F-B (hazard / design limit, confirmed: capstone_dyn_unit.anvil:24-44, capstone_rev_node.anvil:217-234,
  capstone_unit.anvilh:649-651).** DROP on a NONLIN capability that has CDERIVE children invalidates the parent's
  node without freeing it and leaves the children live and unendable: CREVOKE needs a live parent, and nothing else
  ends a NONLIN child. The children stay usable until an MREV handle above the parent, if one exists, is revoked;
  the parent's slot is leaked until then. Not a privilege gain (the children's rights do not grow), but a lifetime
  hole for any allocator that DROPs a region it has derived from. Recommendation: either refuse DROP on a node that
  has children (status 3 -> 29) or make DROP walk like CREVOKE; QEMU's rule decides which (helper lane). Directed
  test: derive, drop the parent, load through the child (expected today: the load succeeds), then CREVOKE (refused
  25).
- **F-C (defect, diagnosability: capstone_dyn_unit.anvil:150-151, 217-218, 265-268, 312-315).** The rev-node
  unit's status 2 means both "pool exhausted" and "parent at the depth maximum", and status 3 ("free list
  corrupt") is reported as cause 25 (a dead operand) by init_req, mrev_req and derive_req, and as 29 by kill_req
  (which also uses 3 for "not a direct child"). Software cannot tell a corrupted list from a stale capability.
  Recommendation: a distinct cause for status 3 (the spec's 30 family or a new one), and a status 4 for the depth
  maximum; cheap, but every test's expected vector that reads 25 at a refusal needs re-reading.
- **F-D (design limit, no reachable trigger found: capstone_rev_node.anvil:20, 106, 524-528).** REVOKE_NODE and
  DEEPEN_NODE terminate on `depth <= bound` while following tree links and have no flush exit inside the walk; a
  cycle in the tree links that stays below the bound would hang the unit uninterruptibly. The corruption detector
  only fires on a free-list pop. No insertion sequence was found that can create such a cycle; a crafted record
  (via F-A's instructions or a simulation poke) is the only way to exercise it. Recommendation: a walk-length
  guard (65,536 steps -> status 3) is one register and one compare; worth it because the alternative is a wedge.
- (Minor) DEEPEN_NODE's write pass rewrites dead descendants too and so re-broadcasts an invalidation for an index
  already dead; harmless.

## 7. Findings, ranked

Ranked by what it costs if left alone, highest first. Each line names its evidence section and who decides.

1. **Debug mints are unrestricted outside supervised domains** (6.1 F-A; decoder.sv:1125-1139). Any
   non-supervised code at any privilege can CAPNODE a live node id onto a fresh register and CAPBOUND it over
   everything. The defended boundary (supervised process domains) holds; a plain-CALL domain is not defended.
   Decision for the lead: fence the group on M-mode or debug mode (one decoder condition; a directed test with an
   S-mode CAPCREATE trapping 2 and the M-mode control passing). Cost of leaving it: the Sublet lifetime model is
   only as strong as the supervision bit on every domain that matters.
2. **The physical-profile QEMU on dev does not renormalise** (6.2, R-54 row; `git diff d35af47b163c 4a21b485b01f
   -- target/riscv/cap_compress.c` is +20 lines). Every QEMU-vs-silicon differential run from dev's pin diverges
   on a region whose rounded span reaches 1024 granules; the 2026-10-16 corpora agreed only because every object
   was exact below 4 KiB. Action (helper lane, no decision needed): land 4a21b485b01f's renormalisation on the
   QEMU shared branch and bump the pin; file the Q- row until then.
3. **DROP on a NONLIN parent leaks its node and strands its children** (6.1 F-B). A lifetime hole for any
   allocator that drops a region it has derived from; not a privilege gain. Decision for the lead, with the helper
   lane's QEMU rule as the tie-breaker: refuse DROP on a node with children (3 -> 29) or make DROP walk like
   CREVOKE. A directed test exists on paper (derive, drop the parent, load through the child succeeds today).
4. **The shipped RTL is on a lane branch and the parent's pointer is four bitstreams stale** (section 1). Every
   `git submodule update` on dev yields RTL from 2026-10-05; nothing flashed since is on any shared branch of
   capstone-ariane. Decision for the lead: the landing shape (fast-forward keeps the 111-commit gated trail; a
   squash hides it), then the pointer bump. R-47's "not yet landed" row is this finding seen from one issue.
5. **The timing operating point is empirical** (section 2). Three bitstreams at -15.3..-15.9 against a band of
   -13.3..-5.9, all functional; the cause (the renormalisation decision in series with the shifters) is
   established and the one candidate that attacks it (fp-renorm-dual 2859dfb67) is gated and never built.
   Decision for the lead: build the candidate (one synthesis slot, ~3 h) or re-base the band on the S-16 set as
   the gate every flash must pass. Leaving both means the next 2 ns lost is noticed by a failed boot.
6. **The registry is a week behind the board** (6.2): 16 rows stale in substance, R-53 and S-11 have no entry,
   R-42/R-34/R-24 still say "not on silicon", Q-07 is resolved and Q-10 no longer aborts, R-13 is refuted by source,
   R-21's closing clause says "nothing to report" while Sublet's authority model depends on it staying open.
   Action (RTL lane, this week, one commit): the row edits in the table above, in the registry's own wording, with
   the ancestry line each rests on. No decision needed.
7. **Status and cause collapse in the rev-node unit** (6.1 F-C): "pool exhausted" and "depth maximum" share status
   2; "free list corrupt" is reported as 25 (a dead operand) by three handlers. Software cannot tell corruption
   from staleness. Action (RTL lane, next Sublet bitstream, batched): a distinct cause for status 3 and a status 4
   for the depth maximum; every expected vector that reads 25 at a refusal re-read first.
8. **An unguarded walk** (6.1 F-D): REVOKE_NODE and DEEPEN_NODE have no step bound and no flush exit; a link cycle

9. **The lint gate compares counts, not names** (section 4): a loop renamed while another vanished passes. Action
