Peak live heap objects in one process against the heap allocations it would make in a year at 10^10 instructions per second (per process, summed over its threads, 365.25-day years). Allocations per instruction are measured under DynamoRIO. A 55-bit lifetime id with 25 slot and 30 generation bits supports at least (2^25 - peak) x 2^30 allocations before a process can run out of slots. The line is that bound for one year, the arrow the gap to the run closest to it.

The 142 runs shown are those of the 152 in the census with a measured allocation rate as well (Redis left out). Without a rate: pyperformance (4), Firefox (2). With 25 slot and 30 generation bits the shortest bound among them is about 65 years, for spectral_norm (pyperformance, PYTHONMALLOC=malloc).
