== Reading ==
- EXACT, board == simulation:
  - sup-fullswitch (17): the full 67-slot exchange round trip;
  - sup-arm (13), sup-armclose (14);
  - sup-sealsize-1024/2048/off16/cur960 (11 each): the seal accepted, canaries intact;
  - sup-sealsize-64/1023/off8 (2 each): S-11 refuses, cause 29 (0x1d), so a seal below 1024 B or off 16-byte
    alignment is refused on silicon;
  - sup-guards (84/84): the supervision guards.
  Twelve images, every reading.
- sup-mtip: exact except reading 1, the reference spin count until MTIP, which is board timing: 0x7a = 122 iterations
  against 243 in simulation. It is below the "never came" ceiling, and the board's mtime runs at 1 MHz. Every other
  reading is exact, including two timer escapes (csupstatus 3, cause 0x8000000000000007) with MTIP still pending in
  the monitor (0x80) and the quantum escape. The real CLINT timer interrupt escapes a supervised domain on silicon.
- sup-strip x5 (exploratory, invariants only): every variant completes, every mcause reading is 0 or
  0x8000000000000003, and every mepc reading is 0 or inside the image's text.
  - d0 and default: the callee saw no trap during the switch; the caller's handler took the software interrupt
    after return. This is simulation's shape.
  - d8: the same as d0. Simulation's d8 had the callee take it instead.
  - d32 and d128: the callee took the interrupt, as in simulation.
  Which side takes it depends on when the msip write lands; both are legitimate per the test header. The in-switch
  detector is simulation-only, so this test cannot show the hazard's absence on silicon. It shows completion, and
  the absence of any foreign mcause or out-of-text mepc.
- Repeats (N = 3 with 2026-10-02's runs):
  - sup-escape v2: 72/72 three times, saved mcause 0 in all five arms each time;
  - sup-quantum v2 q64 and q16: ladder exact three times, 74 preemptions every time at both quanta;
  - call-retpc: identical three times.
