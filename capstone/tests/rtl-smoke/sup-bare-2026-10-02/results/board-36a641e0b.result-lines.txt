  - 0x1234: the domain's live x1 survived every resume.
  - 0x35 = 53 = s7, the monitor's resume count, which the monitor-side restore must preserve.
  - mie 0x888: the monitor's mie came back.
  - 0xA5 does NOT discriminate here: this stub never writes x30, and the domain inherits the monitor's x2..x31 at

  - mcause 0: no trap was taken.
  - CORRECTION (audit): the board vector is BIT-IDENTICAL to the RTL lane's simulation of the same tree (all7,

    and its "board N may differ" prediction is moot. Likely reason the counts match despite DDR versus the testbench's
    12-cycle memory model: the quantum counts only domain cycles outside the exchange, and the stub runs from the

== What this establishes, and what it does not ==
- Against a bitstream WITHOUT supervised-CALL hardware, sup-quantum and sup-escape discriminate by construction:
  cssupervise and the 0xFC0..0xFC3 CSRs do not exist there.
- Shown on silicon, one run each:
  - quantum preemption with exactly-once resume (aggregate);
  - fault-event delivery with the right cause, epc and tval for five fault classes;
  - the monitor's registers back from its private save area, and the seal handed back SEALED;
  - csrw mepc refused by the supervision CSR gate, and mret by the decoder guard.
- Not shown:
  - that the trap is stripped;
  - repeatability;
  - the MTIP escape, the guards, the hostile domain, and S-11's size and alignment refusal;
  - anything through the OpenSBI monitor or Linux.
  The delegated runtime on silicon still needs the runtime lane's FPGA monitor.
- Instrument note (audit): the runner's GDB fallback does NOT work. Every gdb.txt shows `Unable to halt.
  dmcontrol=0x80000001, dmstatus=0x00030c82` (allrunning): the halt request was not honoured while the core spun in the
  capability-mode report loop. All three runs reported through the UART. A wedged run would have returned nothing.
