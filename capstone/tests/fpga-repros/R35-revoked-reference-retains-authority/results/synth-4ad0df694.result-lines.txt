== WHAT FIXED IT: two defects, not one ==
# f83fe9342's -27.665 had TWO causes, found at pin level by the synthesis lane:
#  (a) ON the path: the rev-node's COMBINATIONAL write-request selector drove the cache's 256-entry
#      write decode. Fixed at a87a24a59 by registering both fill taps.
#  (b) NEXT TO the path: regional routing congestion (+50-60 %), the cache inside the worst window.
#      30 of f83fe9342's worst 500 went PAST the cache and still lost ~16 ns of wire. Fixed at 4ad0df694
#      by moving the tag array into distributed RAM (the reset on every flop was what forbade it).
# Evidence that (b) was real and not just inference: those same paths recovered ~18.5 ns in this build
# WITHOUT BEING TOUCHED. The worst path now ends at issue_read_operands fu_data_q, their endpoint class.
