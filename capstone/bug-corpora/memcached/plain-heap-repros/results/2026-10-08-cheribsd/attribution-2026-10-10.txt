memcached/plain-heap-repros cases 06, 07, 08 on CheriBSD and PoisonCap: which probe contains the recorded fault pc
==============================================================================================================

ANSWER: in all four records the fault pc of 06, 07 and 08 lies INSIDE mch_read_probe, at mch_read_probe+0x10,
and OUTSIDE mch_write_probe. These are read defects caught at the read probe. Positive control: the write
defects 01 and 05 in the same runs fault inside mch_write_probe, so the method separates the two probes.

Method: for each matched run, the case binary on disk was identified by sha256 against the summary's
results[].inputs.target (the summary's "binaries" entry for a buggy arm is the supervise wrapper, not the
case). The symbols come from the CHERI SDK's llvm-nm (<home>/cheri/output/sdk/bin/llvm-nm -S --defined-only).
Loaded ranges = nm address + the "SUPERVISE base 0x100000" line in that arm's own stdout. The fault pc is the
"SUPERVISE fault ... pc=" line in the same stdout.

Why the records say "write probe": at the commits that produced these records, runners/run-cheribsd.sh compiled
supervise with -DPROBE_SYMBOL='"mch_write_probe"' as a compile-time constant (4a288cdef72c line 43;
575c878ede80 line 49), so every arm's "SUPERVISE expect" line names mch_write_probe whatever the case's access is.
Since then HEAD 09cc4392bcc1 (landed 2026-10-10 18:27, while this audit ran) builds one supervisor per probe
(line 54); its comment cites results/2026-10-08-cheribsd/attribution-2026-10-10.txt, which did NOT exist in the
tree when this was checked.

What each committed text claims:
- memcached/plain-heap-repros/results/2026-10-08-cheribsd/README.md quotes "SUPERVISE expect mch_write_probe"
  for 06/07/08; its commit 4a288cdef72c says "the fault lies in the probe's extent"; the 06 case.json oracle
  for 2026-10-08 (case.json at HEAD 09cc4392bcc1, line 59) says "the fault lies inside the probe's extent" with expect mch_write_probe 0x102578.
  WRONG for 06/07/08: each fault is 0x34 bytes past mch_write_probe's start, beyond its 0x24-byte extent,
  and lies in mch_read_probe.
- results/2026-10-09-cheribsd-subobject, -poisoncap-mode0 and -poisoncap-mode1 READMEs (and the case.json
  oracles for those arms) say "fault pc ... OUTSIDE mch_write_probe [..)". That is TRUE but incomplete: none of
  them names mch_read_probe as the function that contains the pc.
- Corroboration already in the tree: the 06/07/08 case.json native ASan rows read "frames=0:mch_read_probe",
  and the Capstone arms read "CAUGHT at mch_read_probe+0x38".
