Side observation (not a length difference): in mode 0 the 24- and 32-byte blocks reuse base 0x40a1a000, the
address the 17-byte block had; on the stock image and in mode 1 they get fresh addresses (0x40a1a020,
0x40a1a040). That looks like immediate re-issue with revocation off; it was not checked against the probe's
source (whether it frees between sizes), so treat it as an observation only.
The cap-bounds binary is the same in both PoisonCap modes (sha256 11c551d7...) and differs from the stock-image
one (c1182ed1...), so each image's reading is from a binary built for it.
