wireshark/wmem-repros PoisonCap cases 16, 17, 18, 20, 21: is the recorded fault in wm_defect_write?
=================================================================================================

ANSWER: YES. In all five cases, in both PoisonCap modes, the fault pc is EXACTLY the wm_defect_write label
(the labelled `sb` store), inside the function wm_write_probe. "wm_defect_probe+0x28" is the same address:
wm_defect_probe sits at wm_probe+0x10, wm_write_probe starts right after wm_probe's 0x24 bytes, and
wm_defect_write sits at wm_write_probe+0x14, so wm_defect_write = wm_defect_probe + 0x28 in every binary.
The committed README's "NOT AT wm_defect_probe" is true but names only the READ label; these are write
crossings caught AT the write label.
Positive control: the read crossings 13, 14, 15 and 19 in the same boot fault AT wm_defect_probe, inside wm_probe,
so the method does separate the two labels.
